The Ram Charitra Manas, written by the saint-poet Goswami Tulsidas between 1574 and 1577 CE, is one of the greatest works of world literature and the most beloved scripture of the Hindu tradition after the Vedas and Upanishads.

Composed in the Avadhi dialect of Hindi — the language of the common people — Tulsidas brought the divine story of Lord Ram out of Sanskrit scholarship and placed it in the hands and hearts of millions. Its verses, called chaupais, are sung, recited, and memorized across India and the diaspora every single day.

Unlike a mere retelling, the Manas is a philosophical and devotional masterwork, embedding within its narrative the deepest truths of dharma, bhakti (devotion), and the path to liberation.

Seven Sacred Books

The Seven Kands

The Manas is divided into seven cantos — each a complete spiritual journey within the larger epic. Together they trace the arc of the divine, from creation to coronation.

1 🌅
Bal Kand
The Book of Childhood

The celestial origin of the epic, Ram's miraculous birth in Ayodhya, his childhood exploits, education under sage Vishwamitra, and his marriage to the divine Sita at Janakpur.

2 🌲
Ayodhya Kand
The Book of Ayodhya

The heartbreaking turn of fate — Ram, Sita, and Lakshman's 14-year exile from the kingdom, the grief of King Dasharatha, and the great departure into the forest.

3 🦌
Aranya Kand
The Book of the Forest

Adventures in the wilderness, meetings with great sages, the demonic Shurpanakha, and the pivotal abduction of Sita by the ten-headed demon king Ravana of Lanka.

4 🐒
Kishkindha Kand
The Book of Kishkindha

Ram's alliance with the Vanara king Sugriva, the might of Hanuman revealed, and the great monkey army's preparations to search the ends of the earth for the missing Sita.

5 🌊
Sundar Kand
The Beautiful Book

Hanuman's legendary leap across the ocean to Lanka, his discovery of Sita in Ashok Vatika, the burning of Lanka, and the return of hope. The most beloved kand, recited for blessings.

6 ⚔️
Lanka Kand
The Book of Lanka

The great war of dharma — the construction of Ram Setu, the epic battle between divine forces and Ravana's army, the slaying of Ravana, and the liberation of Sita.

7 👑
Uttar Kand
The Concluding Book

Ram's triumphant return to Ayodhya, the joyous coronation — Ram Rajya — the ideal kingdom of righteousness. Contains Tulsidas's philosophical dialogues on devotion and liberation.

"मंगल भवन अमंगल हारी, द्रवहु सुदसरथ अजर बिहारी"

"O Lord Ram, destroyer of all inauspiciousness, bestower of blessings — be gracious upon us who sing your eternal glory."

— Opening Invocation, Ram Charitra Manas

The Poet-Saint

Goswami Tulsidas

Born circa 1532 CE in Rajapur (in present-day Uttar Pradesh), Goswami Tulsidas is considered one of the greatest saints and poets in the entire history of Indian literature. His life itself reads like a divine story — early hardship, spiritual awakening, and an unwavering devotion to Lord Ram that transformed him into a vessel for the divine.

Legend holds that Tulsidas received the direct blessings of Hanuman, who led him to a vision of Ram and Lakshman themselves. From that sacred encounter was born the resolve to compose the Manas — to give the world Ram's story in the tongue of the people.

He is said to have composed the Ram Charitra Manas over two years, seven months, and twenty-six days — completing it on the occasion of Ram Navami. Beyond the Manas, he authored dozens of works, including the Hanuman Chalisa, recited by hundreds of millions daily across the world.

"Tulsidas holds Ram in his heart the way a lamp holds its flame — inseparable, radiant, eternal."

Cultural Legacy

Why the Manas Endures

Across five centuries, the Ram Charitra Manas has woven itself into the very fabric of Indian civilization — shaping art, music, ethics, governance, and the human spirit.

📖
Living Scripture

Recited daily in households, temples, and on the banks of the Ganges. The Manas is not a historical text — it is a living conversation with the divine, read and sung every day.

🎭
Ram Leela

The source of the Ram Leela theatrical tradition performed across India during Navratri and Dussehra, bringing Ram's story alive in village squares and grand stages alike.

🎵
Musical Heritage

Its chaupais have inspired classical ragas, bhajans, and folk songs. The Manas has generated a vast musical tradition that spans every regional style of Indian devotional music.

⚖️
Dharmic Ethics

Mahatma Gandhi's concept of Ram Rajya — an ideal society of truth and justice — was directly drawn from the Manas. It remains the moral compass of millions.

🌍
Global Diaspora

Carried by generations of emigrants, the Manas has spread to Mauritius, Trinidad, Fiji, the UK, and the Americas, wherever the Hindu diaspora has made its home.

🧘
Path to Liberation

Beyond story and culture, the Manas is a complete guide to bhakti — devotional love as the supreme means of liberation, accessible to all, regardless of caste or learning.

"जो सुमिरत सिधि होइ गन नायक करिबर बदन। करउ अनुग्रह सोइ बुद्धि रासि सुभ गुन सदन॥"

"He who is remembered bestows all perfection — the leader of Ganas, with the face of an elephant. May he bestow grace — the abode of all good virtues."

— Mangalacharan, Ram Charitra Manas
